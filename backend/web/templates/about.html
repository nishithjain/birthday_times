About

The Birthday Chronicle

The Birthday Chronicle is a personalized keepsake newspaper that tells you what happened in the world on your birthday.

Inspired by vintage newspapers, it combines historical events, famous birthdays, movies, music, sports, and economic data from the year you were born.

Data Sources

  • Historical Events — Wikidata
  • Famous Birthdays — Wikidata
  • Movies — TMDB
  • Economic Data — World Bank
